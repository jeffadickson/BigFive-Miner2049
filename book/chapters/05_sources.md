# Sources {#sources}

Facts about the Atari hardware, its history and the game's history come from these sources. Everything about how the program works comes from the disassembly itself, the emulator and the harness.

- [An Interview with Bill Hogue](https://www.trs-80.org/interview-bill-hogue.html), trs-80.org (TRS-80 tools, the Model 16 cross-assembler, the Z80 and LDIR).
- [Miner 2049er](https://www.trs-80.org/miner-2049er.html), trs-80.org (the unreleased TRS-80 version, the 80 Micro interview, ports).
- [Bill Hogue interview](https://www.ataricompendium.com/archives/interviews/bill_hogue/interview_bill_hogue.html), The Atari Compendium (Radio Shack Reseda, CSUN, the name Big Five, licensing).
- [ANTIC Interview 94: Bill Hogue, Miner 2049er](https://ataripodcast.libsyn.com/antic-interview-94-bill-hogue-miner-2049er), ANTIC The Atari 8-bit Podcast, 2015.

- [Miner 2049er](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miner_2049er), Wikipedia.
- [Super Nova (video game)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Nova_(video_game)), Wikipedia; and a disassembly of SNOVA11C.CMD made for an earlier volume in this series.
- Owen W. Linzmayer, [Atari strikes gold](https://www.atarimagazines.com/creative/v9n2/128_Atari_strikes_gold.php), Creative Computing, February 1983.
- John J. Anderson, [Mastering Miner 2049er](https://atarimagazines.com/cva/v1n2/miner.php), Creative Computing Video & Arcade Games, Fall 1983.
